Google Forms is a fine internal tool. For customer-facing forms where brand and conversion matter, it shows its limits fast.

FAQ

Common questions

Isn't Google Forms completely free with unlimited responses?

Yes — Google Forms is free and has no response caps, which is a real advantage. If you only need a basic internal form and don't care about branding, analytics, or conversion, Google Forms is a solid choice. But if your forms are customer-facing, FormflowX gives you branded, conversion-optimised forms. The free plan covers 100 responses/month — enough to validate and test before upgrading.

Can I get a custom domain with Google Forms?

No. Google Forms always live on docs.google.com or forms.gle URLs. There is no way to serve a Google Form on your own domain. FormflowX Pro ($15/mo) includes custom domain support via a simple CNAME record.

Is FormflowX better for lead generation than Google Forms?

Yes. The one-at-a-time conversational UX dramatically improves completion rates compared to Google Forms' traditional layout. FormflowX also captures partial submissions — so if someone drops off halfway, you still have their email. Google Forms captures nothing from incomplete submissions.

Can I import a Google Form to FormflowX?

Yes. FormflowX supports importing from Google Forms. Log in and go to New form → Import to migrate your questions and logic automatically.

Does FormflowX work for internal team forms too?

Absolutely. FormflowX works just as well for internal forms — HR onboarding, bug reports, team feedback — as for customer-facing ones. The difference is that when you want to go branded and public-facing, FormflowX is already built for it.
