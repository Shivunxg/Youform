Frequently asked questions

Are multi-step forms free in FormflowX?

Yes. Multi-step mode is available on all plans including the free tier. Convert any form to one-question-per-screen in a single click from the form builder settings.

Do multi-step forms actually improve completion rates?

Consistently, yes. Showing one question at a time reduces perceived form length and cognitive load. Research across thousands of forms shows completion rates 40–120% higher compared to long-scroll equivalents. The exact lift depends on form length and topic.

Can I combine multi-step with conditional logic?

Yes, and it's one of the most powerful combinations in FormflowX. Build a form where each screen is a step, and logic rules skip or show steps based on earlier answers — respondents experience a tailored path through the form.

Are partial submissions captured in multi-step forms?

Yes. FormflowX captures answers at each step as the respondent progresses. If they drop off on step 4 of 8, you still have their answers from steps 1–3. This is especially valuable for longer intake forms and lead gen flows.
