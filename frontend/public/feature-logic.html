What you can build with conditional logic

B2B vs B2C funnels

Qualification forms

Only ask follow-up questions when a respondent qualifies. Skip detailed service questions when the budget is below your minimum.

Multi-product intake

"If NPS score ≤ 6 → show: what went wrong?"

Rule types

Logic rules that cover real-world complexity

Show / Hide

Show or hide questions

Based on a previous answer, reveal or hide any question. The form re-flows smoothly so respondents never see a blank space.

Jump to

Skip ahead in the form

Send respondents to a specific question or the end of the form — ideal for short-circuiting paths that don't apply.

AND / OR

Multiple conditions

Combine conditions: show a question only if answer A is "Yes" AND answer B is greater than 5. OR logic lets either condition trigger the rule.

Comparisons

Rich comparison operators

Equals, does not equal, contains, starts with, is greater than, is less than — for text, number, and choice answers.

Frequently asked questions

Is conditional logic free in FormflowX?

Yes. Show/hide logic and jump rules are available on all plans including the free tier. There's no upgrade required to build branching forms.

What types of logic conditions does FormflowX support?

You can build rules based on: equals / does not equal, contains / does not contain, starts with, is greater than / less than (for numbers and ratings), and is empty / is not empty. Rules can use AND or OR to combine multiple conditions.

Can I use logic with any question type?

You can trigger logic from answers to: multiple choice, dropdown, short text, number, rating, and date questions. You can apply show/hide rules to any question type in the form.

Is there a limit on how many logic rules I can add?

No. There's no limit on the number of rules per form or across your account. Build as many branches as your use case requires.
