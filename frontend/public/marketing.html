● FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Is the free plan really free forever?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 with no credit card required. You get 100 responses/month and 3 forms permanently — no trial period, no expiry date, no catch.

How is FormflowX different from Typeform?

Typeform charges per response on most plans — your bill grows with your audience. FormflowX doesn't. You get a fixed monthly quota regardless of how many forms you run. Conditional logic, partial submissions, and analytics are also available on lower-tier plans.

Can I connect FormflowX to Google Sheets?

Yes. FormflowX integrates directly with Google Sheets so new responses appear as rows automatically. Pro and Business plans also support Slack, Zapier, custom Webhooks, and more integrations.

Can I remove the FormflowX branding?

Yes, on the Pro plan and above. You can also use a custom domain so your forms appear on your own URL with no FormflowX branding anywhere — respondents never know what's powering the form.

What happens if I exceed my monthly response limit?

On the Free plan, forms pause accepting new responses until the next month resets. On paid plans you can upgrade for more capacity at any time. We never charge you per response — your bill never surprises you.

Is my data secure and GDPR compliant?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. FormflowX is GDPR compliant — you can export or permanently delete your account data at any time from Account Settings. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I import forms from Typeform or Google Forms?

Yes. FormflowX supports one-click import from both Typeform and Google Forms. Your questions, conditional logic, and answer options migrate automatically — no rebuilding from scratch.
