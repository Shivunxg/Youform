Frequently asked questions

How does the conditional routing work in this template?

Based on the overall star rating: 1–3 stars triggers "What could we do better?" and 4–5 stars triggers the testimonial capture question. Both paths then converge on the optional contact email question. You can adjust these thresholds in the logic editor.

Can I collect testimonials and permission to use them in the same form?

Yes. The template includes a testimonial text field and a checkbox asking "May we use this as a testimonial?" Only customers who rate 4–5 stars see this section — so you're only asking happy customers.

Can I get an immediate alert for negative feedback?

Yes. Set up a Slack integration or Zapier filter on the score field: "only continue if rating ≤ 2". Your team receives a message with the score and open-ended comment — full context to follow up immediately.

Is this template free?

Yes. The feedback template is free to use on all plans including the free tier (up to 100 responses/month). Conditional logic and star ratings are included on every plan.
