Frequently asked questions

What makes a lead form convert better?

The biggest levers are: (1) ask for email on the first screen, (2) use multi-step layout so the form doesn't look long, (3) enable partial capture so drop-offs aren't total losses, (4) use conditional logic to skip questions that don't apply. FormflowX supports all four.

Can FormflowX capture leads from people who don't finish?

Yes. Partial submission capture is a Pro feature. When enabled, FormflowX saves the answers from every completed step — even if the respondent never clicks Submit. If they gave their email on step 1, that email is captured regardless of what happens on steps 2–5.

How does drop-off analytics work?

FormflowX tracks how many respondents reach each step and how many exit at each step. You'll see a drop-off chart per form showing exactly which question has the highest exit rate. Fix that question — reword it, move it later, or remove it — and watch overall completion rates improve.

Is FormflowX free for lead generation?

Yes. You can build and publish unlimited lead gen forms on the free plan with up to 100 responses per month. Partial submission capture, drop-off analytics, and unlimited responses require the Pro plan ($15/month).
