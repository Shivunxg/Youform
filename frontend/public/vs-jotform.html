FAQ

Common questions about switching from Jotform

Does FormflowX support the same field types as Jotform?

FormflowX covers all the essential field types: short text, long text, email, number, dropdown, multiple choice, date, file upload, rating, and more. Jotform has 40+ field types including payment, signature, and appointment fields. If you need highly specialised inputs, Jotform may cover more edge cases — but for lead capture, surveys, onboarding, and intake forms, FormflowX has everything you need.

Can I migrate from Jotform to FormflowX?

Yes. You can manually recreate your forms in FormflowX in minutes — the builder is much faster to use. We're also working on a direct import tool. Log in and go to New form to get started.

What makes one-at-a-time forms better?

Showing all questions at once increases perceived effort and causes people to abandon before they start. One-at-a-time forms are psychologically easier — each step feels small and achievable. This is the same technique used by the best onboarding flows and consumer apps. FormflowX is built entirely around this principle.

Does FormflowX have drop-off analytics?

Yes. On the Pro plan, FormflowX shows you exactly which question causes abandonment — with per-question drop-off rates. This tells you where to improve your form copy, reorder questions, or remove fields. Jotform does not offer this feature.

Is FormflowX good for agencies managing multiple clients?

Yes. FormflowX Pro and Business plans include custom domain support, branding removal, and workspace management — so you can run forms under your client's brand. We're also adding white-label support. If you manage forms for multiple clients, the Business plan at $39/mo gives you the best value.
