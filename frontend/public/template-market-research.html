Frequently asked questions

What question types are available in FormflowX?

Short text, long text, multiple choice (single and multi-select), dropdown, star rating, number, date, file upload, and Likert-style scales. All are available on the free plan and can be combined in any order.

Can I segment responses by demographic in Google Sheets?

Yes. Since each response is a row in your Sheet, you can filter, sort, and pivot by any column — including role, company size, or industry. The template is designed so demographic columns appear first, making segmentation straightforward.

Can I use this for product validation / Jobs-to-be-Done research?

Yes. The template covers the core structure: current situation, pain level, existing solutions, willingness to pay, and feature prioritisation. Replace the placeholder text with your specific product context and you're ready to run.

How many responses can I collect for free?

The free plan allows up to 100 responses per month. The Pro plan ($15/month) removes the response cap and adds drop-off analytics and partial submission capture — useful for longer research runs.
